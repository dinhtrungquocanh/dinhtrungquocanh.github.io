Research

My key research questions:

1. What key factors govern the star formation in dusty merging systems at Cosmic Noon?
2. What are the morphologies and size-mass relation of galaxies in a high-z protocluster?
3. How did massive galaxies at high redshifts assemble their stellar mass?
4. What are the effects of non-parametric/parametric models on reconstructing the star formation history?

Bachelor Thesis: Star Formation History of Massive Galaxies at z > 3

Advisor: Dr. Abdurro'uf (Indiana University)

In my bachelor thesis, I constructed a sample of 80 massive galaxies, from CEERS and PRIMER surveys. I compared between their non-parametric and parametric star formation histories (SFHs) using Bagpipes. From the galaxies' non-parametric SFHs, I found that they were able to assemble their stellar mass in rapid timescales. I also detected a subsample of massive quiescent galaxies that suppress their star formation and some rejuvenating candidates that re-ignite the star-forming process. About SEAC and VAAN

Space Engineering's Astronomy Club (SEAC) is a student club of the Space Engineering major at International University, Vietnam National University - HCMC. I was the president of SEAC from 2023-2025 and now I'm an advisor for the club. We hold weekly meetings to discuss different astronomy topics: from the Solar system to the Universe's origin. In each semester, we also organize starwatch events, academic seminars and off-campus trips for our members.
Our club is also a member of the Vietnam Amateur Astronomy Network (VAAN). Along with other astronomy clubs, we organize stem activities and stargazing events for the community.
