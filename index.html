About Me

Hi, my name is Quốc Anh /Kuək An/ from Dong Nai, Vietnam. I have just completed my bachelor degree in Space Engineering at HCMIU. Currently, I am an independent researcher working on star formation of high-redshift galaxies in extreme environments. I hope to become a professional astrophysics researcher and educator someday.
My other interests are clouds, chess, geology, song-writing, pop culture & questioning the existence of things.
